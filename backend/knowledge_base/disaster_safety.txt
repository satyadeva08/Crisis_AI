FLOOD SAFETY AND RESCUE

Floods can cause drowning, electrical hazards, structural damage, contaminated water, blocked roads, and vehicle entrapment.

Immediate flood safety actions:
- Move affected people to higher and safer ground.
- Do not walk through moving flood water.
- Do not drive through flooded roads.
- Avoid contact with electrical equipment or fallen electrical wires.
- Deploy rescue boats when people are stranded in areas inaccessible by road.
- Prioritize trapped people, children, elderly people, injured people, and people requiring medical assistance.
- Establish evacuation routes away from rapidly rising water.
- Coordinate rescue teams with local emergency authorities.
- Provide medical assistance to injured or exposed victims.
- Avoid entering damaged buildings until they are assessed for structural safety.

Flood rescue resources:
- Rescue boats
- Trained rescue personnel
- Medical teams
- First-aid kits
- Life jackets
- Ropes and rescue equipment
- Emergency communication equipment
- Transportation for evacuation

Flood severity indicators:
- Rapidly rising water
- People trapped inside buildings
- Submerged vehicles
- People requiring medical assistance
- Roads becoming inaccessible
- Electrical hazards
- Structural damage
- Large numbers of affected people

If people are trapped inside houses or vehicles:
- Treat the incident as high priority.
- Deploy appropriate rescue personnel and equipment.
- Use boats when road access is unavailable.
- Move rescued people to safe elevated locations.
- Provide medical assessment after rescue.

EARTHQUAKE SAFETY

Earthquakes can cause building collapse, falling objects, fires, damaged utilities, injuries, and people becoming trapped.

Immediate earthquake safety actions:
- Move away from damaged structures.
- Protect yourself from falling objects.
- Prioritize people trapped under debris.
- Avoid damaged electrical wires and gas lines.
- Establish a safe evacuation area.
- Deploy search and rescue teams.
- Provide medical assistance to injured people.

Earthquake rescue resources:
- Search and rescue teams
- Medical teams
- First-aid equipment
- Rescue tools
- Communication equipment
- Structural assessment teams

CYCLONE SAFETY

Cyclones can cause strong winds, flooding, structural damage, fallen trees, power failures, and injuries.

Immediate cyclone safety actions:
- Move people to designated shelters or safe buildings.
- Keep people away from windows and damaged structures.
- Evacuate areas at risk of flooding.
- Avoid fallen electrical wires.
- Deploy rescue teams after dangerous conditions are assessed.
- Prioritize injured and vulnerable people.

Cyclone rescue resources:
- Emergency shelters
- Rescue teams
- Medical teams
- Emergency communication equipment
- Transportation
- First-aid supplies

LANDSLIDE SAFETY

Landslides can bury roads, buildings, and people and can cause secondary landslides.

Immediate landslide safety actions:
- Keep people away from unstable slopes.
- Evacuate areas at risk of additional movement.
- Deploy search and rescue teams.
- Check for trapped victims.
- Avoid entering unstable structures.
- Establish safe access routes.

Landslide rescue resources:
- Search and rescue teams
- Medical teams
- Excavation equipment
- Communication equipment
- Transportation
